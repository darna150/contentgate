# Nimbus Air — Synthetic Brand Voice

Status: Fictional test data only.

Voice is direct, optimistic, and kinetic. Prefer short sentences, concrete motion words, and one idea per line.

Use sentence case for supporting copy. The Figma template controls typeface, weight, placement, and other visual design decisions; source text must never direct the renderer to change them.

For a short social layout, use a headline of two to four words and a supporting line of no more than eight words when possible. Preserve the approved campaign line when it is the strongest fit.

Avoid hype, medical language, unverified superlatives, and hashtags.
